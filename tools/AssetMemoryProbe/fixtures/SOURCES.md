# JPEG fixture sources

These are original Lucent test patterns generated by
[`GenerateFixtures.py`](../GenerateFixtures.py), distributed under the repository's MIT license.
They contain only arithmetic gradients and checker patterns; no photographs or third-party
artwork are included. They are test inputs and are not packaged into Lucent products.

The checked-in bytes were generated with Pillow 12.3.0 and libjpeg-turbo 3.1.4.1.
Regeneration is optional and requires that maintainer environment. Ordinary .NET builds and
measurements consume the pinned files and do not require Python.
